- statement: On an HCP-200 standard design, the impeller-to-casing clearance spec is 0.38 to 0.50 mm diametral.
- statement: To check it, measure the clearance between the impeller and wear ring with a feeler gauge at four points 90° apart.
- statement: When you are troubleshooting low flow or low head, if the pump makes less head at the same flow than it did at commissioning, the hydraulics have worn, and if the suction pressure has dropped, the problem is upstream of the pump.

- step: 3. Read the alarm history on the controller and note which circuit alarmed, when it alarmed, and the suction and discharge pressures at the time.
- step: 4. Rule out non-leak causes of low suction pressure: an iced evaporator, low chilled water flow, a dirty condenser coil, a blocked filter drier with temperature drop across the drier above 3 C, or an expansion valve not modulating.
- step: 5. Inspect for oil stains at joints, flare nuts, service valves and the compressor, because oil marks where refrigerant escapes.
- step: 6. Run the unit and leak test both circuits with a calibrated electronic leak detector with sensitivity 3 g/year or better, moving the probe no faster than 5 cm per second and below joints because R-513A is heavier than air.
- step: 7. Confirm every indication with bubble solution before you open the circuit.
- step: 8. If no leak is found but the charge is low, do not top up; record the result, tag the unit, and escalate to the team lead for an ultrasonic or tracer-gas test.

- step: 11. Repair the leak, and replace the filter drier core 63-0410 whenever the circuit has been opened.
- step: 12. Fit the gauge manifold and nitrogen regulator, and never pressurise directly from the nitrogen cylinder without a regulator.
- step: 13. Pressurise in steps to 3 bar and check for audible leaks, then to 10 bar and check with bubble solution.
- step: 14. Pressurise with dry nitrogen to 15 bar, hold for 30 minutes, and record the start and end pressure and the ambient temperature at start and end.
- step: 15. Release the nitrogen to atmosphere outdoors.
- step: 16. Connect a vacuum pump with short, large-bore hoses, remove the Schrader cores, evacuate to below 500 microns, then isolate the pump and hold for 15 minutes.
