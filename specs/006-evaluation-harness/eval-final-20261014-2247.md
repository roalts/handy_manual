Question: I need to clean the foot strainer in pump pit PP-1 at Fresno. How do I enter the pit safely?

- statement: PP-1 is a permit-required confined space raw-water pit, and for Fresno you enter it for foot strainer cleaning, suction valve work, and suction line leaks; do not reach into PP-1 through the open hatch with your head below deck level, because that is entry.
- warning: Before you enter, you must have confined-space certification set on the work order, supervisor approval with both names assigned, and the site confined-space permit requested at least 48 hours ahead.
- step: 1. Check the gas monitor is in calibration and bump test it on the day of the entry before you leave for site.
- step: 2. Check the rescue equipment: tripod, winch, full-body harness, retrieval line, and ventilation blower with ducting.
- step: 3. Electrically isolate and lock every pump that discharges into, draws from, or stands in the pit; for PP-1, close and lock raw water supply valve V-101 and the suction isolation valve, then drain the suction line to the pit before entry.
- step: 4. Record every lock on the permit.
- step: 5. Test the atmosphere before anyone breaks the plane of the hatch, from outside the pit, at three levels: just below the hatch, at mid-depth, and 0.3 m above the floor, holding the tube at each level for at least two minutes.
- statement: For Fresno pits, acceptable entry readings are oxygen 19.5% to 23.5%, hydrogen sulphide below 10 ppm, carbon monoxide below 25 ppm, flammable gas below 10% LEL, and carbon dioxide below 5,000 ppm.
- warning: If any reading is outside those limits, ventilate and test again; if the readings are still outside the limits after 30 minutes of ventilation, do not enter, close the hatch, inform Sierra Springs EHS, and call the entry supervisor and the team lead.
- step: 6. Run the ventilation blower with the duct to 0.5 m above the pit floor for at least 10 minutes before the final pre-entry test, keep it running for the whole entry, place the blower inlet upwind and away from the truck yard, and do not use pure oxygen or compressed air from the plant system to ventilate.
- step: 7. When the readings are acceptable, have the entry supervisor complete and sign the Corvane permit and post it at the hatch next to the site permit; the permit is valid for one shift, at most 8 hours, and only for the work and people named on it.
- step: 8. Wear a full-body harness with the retrieval line attached to the dorsal ring, and keep the line attached to the winch for the whole entry; wear the personal gas monitor in the breathing zone.
- step: 9. For PP-1, use the non-entry rescue setup with the tripod centred over the 900 mm hatch and the winch on the leg facing the pump house door.
- warning: If the work position makes a straight lift impossible and no second anchor can be set, do not go ahead with the entry; for PP-1, straight lift from all work positions is listed as possible with no limitation known.
- step: 10. Have the attendant log you in with the time, keep at least five-minute contact, and make sure the attendant watches the blower, the monitor display where visible, and the yard.
- warning: Leave the pit at once if the monitor alarms, the attendant tells you to leave, you feel dizzy, short of breath or unwell, or anything changes in the pit, including rising water, smell, or noise.
